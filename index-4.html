About Nova Academy

Nova Academy is an online educational platform created to make practical skill development more accessible for learners. The academy focuses on digital, creative and modern skills that can be learned online from the comfort of home.

Through structured courses, short workshops and educational resources, Nova Academy aims to encourage learners to keep learning, improve their abilities and turn knowledge into practical skills.
